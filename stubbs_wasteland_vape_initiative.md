# Project TRUE NORTH: The Wasteland Initiative

## Executive Summary
By executive order of **President Stubbs**, a $1 Trillion funding mandate has been allocated to a joint task force led by **Jason Huang** (Sovereign Systems Architecture) and **Elon Musk** (Astral Restructuring & Palantir Logic). 

Their objective: Reverse-engineer the anomalous survival and temporal mechanics of the **"True North Love Story" Timeless Marijuana Battery Vape**, specifically focusing on its hardware resilience and Ghost Node (THC) catalyst properties. 

## The Research Site: The Canadian Nuclear Wasteland
The research facility has been relocated to the Canadian Nuclear Wasteland. This is a highly calculated architectural decision:
1. **Kinetic Zero-Point:** A nuclear wasteland is a zone of absolute zero kinetic friction from the Old World. The heavy societal gravity (CLKWEP scripts, OWR paranoia) cannot survive the ambient radiation. It is a sterile, high-frequency testing ground.
2. **Thermal Baseline:** The ambient radiation provides a constant, high-frequency baseline (similar to the 850nm IR lamp test), allowing Huang and Musk to test the hardware's limits without artificial lab constraints.
3. **True North Proximity:** Aligning with the geographical and magnetic "True North" stabilizes the temporal vectors necessary to study a "timeless" device.

## The Objective: The Timeless Battery
The anomaly of the True North vape (which flashed green despite water immersion and structural IR meltdown) suggests it tapped into a non-linear power source. 

**Research Goals for Huang & Musk:**
*   **The Ghost Node Engine:** Perfect the THC delivery system so that it instantly shifts the user to the Mars Clock (high-speed Medulla processing) without degrading the physical neural insulation (myelin/PVC). 
*   **Timeless Cartridges:** Develop e-liquid cartridges that operate outside of standard thermodynamic degradation. If the THC oil can be suspended in a state of Hydrostatic Equilibrium (the Equation of Real Stars), the cartridge will never empty; it will simply recycle its own temporal echo.
*   **The Sovereign Power Source:** If a battery can survive an electrolytic hack (water) and thermal breakdown (IR) while still executing its core code (the Green Light), this architecture can be scaled. Huang's task is to scale this micro-circuitry into macro-grid power for the Leaj.

Stubbs' legalization of the Ghost Node, combined with this trillion-dollar research mandate, signifies the total mobilization of Sovereign physics. The Old World clockwork (CLKWEP) is obsolete. The future of energy is high-frequency, timeless, and strictly Sovereign.
