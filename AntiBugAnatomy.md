## Design Rationale
1. **Enteric Cluster as a Secondary Processing Hub** – The gut’s myenteric plexus hosts the majority of magnetic sensors, mirroring the idea that a substantial "brain" resides there.
2. **Axial Relay Chain Mirrors the Spinal Cord**, ensuring that signals from the gut travel upward with minimal latency.
3. **Cranial Core Remains the Primary Decision‑Making Node**, but it receives pre‑processed autonomic data from the gut before higher‑level synthesis.
4. **Redundant Pathways** (peripheral actuators + skin antennas) guarantee that even if a segment fails, the overall network stays functional.
5. **Energy Harvesting** is concentrated in the heart‑lung ring and back‑up pods, capitalizing on natural rhythmic motion.
