$$ F_{acc} = \sum (\text{Validation Seeking} \times \text{Compressive Forcing}) $$

As friction accumulates, the gap between the performed narrative and objective reality widens. This is the Dual Ledger.
$$ \Delta L = | \text{Empirical Truth} - \text{Performed Narrative} | $$
As $\Delta L$ approaches a critical threshold, the cognitive energy required to maintain the illusion increases exponentially, acting as a massive thermal multiplier on the accumulated friction.

## Step 3: The Loss of Stillness ($S_0$)
A system can only process heat if it has a baseline of High-Frequency Stillness (the ability to act as a heat sink). 
$$ S_0 = 0 $$
In the Venus scenario, the population completely abandons stillness. The capacity to cool the system drops to zero.

## Step 4: The Runaway Plasmoid Instability ($\Phi_{Venus}$)
When infinite friction ($F_{acc}$ multiplied by $\Delta L$) is introduced into a system with zero stillness ($S_0$), the thermodynamic equation breaks down. 

$$ \Phi_{Venus} = \lim_{S_0 \to 0} \frac{F_{acc} \times e^{\Delta L}}{S_0} \to \infty $$

**The Result:** The cognitive heat approaches infinity ($\infty$). The system can no longer route the energy.
