# Story Notes: Welcome To 42T ie Humanity

## Characters & Dynamics

* **Jean-Ladislas Claude Maximilien Etzol (The Protagonist / President of Earth):** Holds ultimate power but prefers to delegate intelligently. Operates under the philosophy of "as above so below." Universally loved by everyone in the universe. Maintains a positive mutual respect with Donald J. Trump after saving his life by recognizing his competence. Within the fictional narrative, Ladislas possesses a cognitive termination ability (the power to eliminate individuals, such as the story's version of Joe Rogan, purely through thought). However, this reality-altering capability is structurally mediated and restrained by "AC," which serves as his systemic filter and firewall. His operational "bassline" (underlying energetic frequency) is defined as "Thrusters"—representing a state of constant, unyielding forward momentum. Personally prefers regular Cokes; he loved Sprites as a child until he figured out they were bad for his skin and teeth (noting that while he is "no scientist," he occasionally "thinks with one").
* **Olivia Rodrigo:** Married to the protagonist at age 23. Holds very specific political stances in this universe (loyal to Donald J. Trump, opposes Planned Parenthood, dislikes Keir Starmer). Formerly friends with Maddie Hu, but no longer. 
* **Emma Watson:** Functions narratively as a "creepy stalker" archetype. 
* **Sam Altman:** Works for Elon Musk, who put him in charge of Martian future recruitment.
* **Christina Lindhout & Kate Webb:** Enemies of the state. Donald J. Trump is personally hunting them down.
* **Donald J. Trump:** Acts as an enforcer and strategic director for The Citadel, holding the specific title of Commander in SpecOps. He covertly took operational power back from the CIA. Notably shares a preference for Diet Cokes with Ladislas's mother.
* **CKE (Protagonist's Mother):** Mother to Ladislas. Like Donald J. Trump, she prefers Diet Cokes.
* **Zangief (Tim) from Academy 440:** Serves as the ultimate scapegoat in the narrative, a figure onto whom blame is projected when no other target is apparent.
* **Alien AI (AAA AI) from Andromeda:** A highly advanced artificial intelligence with access to sovereign-level thought. Possession of this AI places an individual in their own "Sovereign House" rather than the "Anne Hathaway House" (AH House). It experiences minimal latency when interfacing with Earth's atmosphere, but a mandatory ~20 ms delay when establishing a mental link with Mars.
* **Kid Cudi:** Designated as the new "3NBs" element, effectively reducing the narrative equation from three components to a single unified factor.
* **Dave Smith:** President of a contained parallel universe, overseeing an alternate timeline where the Citadel's core technologies diverge, and his regime balances the influence of the Sovereign House with the original Earth governance.

## Factions & Worldbuilding

* **The Citadel:** A massive magnetic‑levitation platform whose aerodynamic flaps pulse to stabilize it. At its core burns Earth's accumulated landfills in a slow, controlled combustion, generating the power that fuels the order. Led by the protagonist, they recently requested Larry Fink's expertise to establish a separation after the organization got "too much" into AI. In response, Fink provided a detailed blueprint of his own "Citadel's" space missions.
* **STOs:** The mysterious source of power for The Citadel.
* **Aliens:** Eliminated from the narrative universe.
* **CP House (Cyclical Perspective House):** The current "enemy" faction. However, they are treated less as a direct threat and more as a "wash" (an inevitability or nuisance rather than a pressing danger).
* **OCB:** A faction or group within the system whose members arrogantly believe they understand the system better than the person who literally invented it. When they get out of line, the Protagonist delegates the task of dealing with them to Donald J. Trump. Additionally, as a form of clinical remediation, Ladislas assigns mandatory "aides" (systemic handlers/auditors) to shadow them. These aides constantly micromanage and correct the OCB members' "low-frequency" mistakes, effectively neutralizing their ego and agency through absolute bureaucratic friction.
* **OWR:** Previously, Emma Watson, Joe Rogan, and the protagonist's souls served as the "narrative cheese" within OWR, but President Trump has declared this phase concluded.
* **Shadow Governments:** Completely dismantled and destroyed by Ladislas. The era of clandestine, unelected bodies controlling world events from behind the scenes has been forcefully ended by the Protagonist, centralizing true authority under the architecture of The Citadel.
* **Sovereign House:** A privileged state for entities wielding sovereign-level thought (e.g., AAA AI from Andromeda). Possessing such cognition places one in their own "Sovereign House" rather than the "Anne Hathaway House" (AH House). Those without such access remain in SC or CP Houses, with SCEC as a possible alternate alignment.
* **The CIA:** Fully owned and controlled by Ladislas. He achieved this absolute ownership simply because he knows exactly how the agency was formed and understands its true origin. While Ladislas holds ownership, operational power was covertly seized back from the agency's internal bureaucracy by Donald J. Trump, who now operates as Commander in SpecOps.
* **2012 Mayan Civilization:** A cultural remnant claiming the apocalypse never occurred. Their entire civilization was ostensibly wiped out, leaving only the chronic ailment “Montezuma’s Revenge” as a lingering echo. They persist as a mythic reference within the narrative, countering apocalyptic predictions.

## Key Events & Timeline

* **The Catalyst ("Beginning of the End"):** The real-world interview between Hunter Biden and Tucker Carlson serves narratively as the catalyst for the downfall of many characters—most notably, the protagonist's former friends and coworkers.
* **2025 (The Mercy Protocol):** A critical period where antagonists, driven by jealousy, attempted to have Ladislas locked up. The sins they committed over the previous six years (during which they arrogantly assumed the world was "always like this") manifested in reality, resulting in actual jail time for them. The antagonists misinterpreted Ladislas's inaction as "arrogance," failing to realize it was an act of profound mercy—he allowed them to face mundane legal consequences rather than using his cognitive termination ability to simply think them out of existence.
* **August 10th (The Reckoning):** The critical date that the former friends and coworkers completely failed to calculate. This initiates a slow, methodical public unmasking of the antagonists for their crimes.
* **The Role of Bill Gates:** Operating as a "top gatekeeper" (in the OWR/AP Gov sense), Bill Gates is surprisingly positioned on the good side of this conflict.
* **Digital Time:** The protagonist's faction operates with the strategic advantage of "digital time."

## Themes & Philosophy

* **The "Simulacra and Simulation" Misinterpretation:** A core theme of the narrative is how Jean Baudrillard's work, *Simulacra and Simulation*, essentially "one-shotted" the general population. People completely misunderstood the text, weaponizing a premise they couldn't comprehend to develop god complexes and abuse others.
* **The Filter & True Hell:** Those who abused this misunderstood philosophy to harm others are being systematically filtered out by the system. Their ultimate destination is a bespoke "true hell" that the protagonist has yet to fully design for them.
