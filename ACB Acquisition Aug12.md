# ASSET PURCHASE AND ACQUISITION AGREEMENT

**THIS ACQUISITION AGREEMENT** (the "Agreement") is made and entered into as of this 11th day of August, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between:

**BUYER:** The Citadel Holdings LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (hereinafter referred to as "Purchaser"), and

**SELLER:** Atlantic City Ballet Inc., a New Jersey non-profit corporation (hereinafter referred to as "Target" or "Seller").

## RECITALS

**WHEREAS**, Seller owns and operates the performing arts institution known as the Atlantic City Ballet, including all associated physical assets, intellectual property, rehearsal facilities, and active employment contracts; and

**WHEREAS**, Purchaser desires to acquire one hundred percent (100%) of the assets, properties, and goodwill of Seller, and Seller desires to sell the same to Purchaser, upon the terms and conditions set forth herein.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 1: PURCHASE AND SALE OF ASSETS

**1.1 Acquired Assets.** 
Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, at the Closing, Seller shall sell, convey, assign, transfer, and deliver to Purchaser all of Seller’s right, title, and interest in and to all assets used in the operation of the Atlantic City Ballet, including but not limited to:
(a) All real estate leases and rehearsal facility deeds;
(b) All intellectual property, trademarks, performance rights, and choreographic copyrights;
(c) All costumes, staging equipment, lighting rigs, and physical inventory;
(d) All active employment and performance contracts for current principal dancers, soloists, and corps de ballet.

## ARTICLE 2: PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Consideration.** 
The total aggregate purchase price for the Acquired Assets shall be **Twenty Million United States Dollars ($20,000,000.00 USD)** (the "Purchase Price").

**2.2 Payment Schedule.** 
The Purchase Price shall be payable as follows:
(a) An initial earnest money deposit of Two Million Dollars ($2,0,000,000.00) payable via wire transfer upon the execution of this Agreement;
(b) The remaining balance of Eighteen Million Dollars ($18,000,000.00) payable in full at Closing.

## ARTICLE 3: CLOSING AND POST-CLOSING COVENANTS

**3.1 Closing Date.** 
The consummation of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "Closing") shall take place at the corporate offices of Purchaser no later than thirty (30) days following the Effective Date.

**3.2 Employment Continuity.** 
Purchaser agrees to honor all existing contracts for current dancers and artistic staff for a minimum period of one (1) performance season following the Closing Date. Purchaser reserves the right to implement new "Kinematic/Performance Enhancements" following the conclusion of the protected season.

**3.3 Assumption of Liabilities.** 
Purchaser shall not assume, nor be liable for, any debts, obligations, or liabilities of Seller incurred prior to the Effective Date, except those expressly assumed in writing.

## ARTICLE 4: GENERAL PROVISIONS

**4.1 Governing Law.** 
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New Jersey, without giving effect to any choice of law principles.

**4.2 Entire Agreement.** 
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes any prior understandings, agreements, or representations by or between the Parties, written or oral, to the extent they relate in any way to the subject matter hereof.
