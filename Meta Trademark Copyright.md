# Meta – A Conceptual Definition

**Meta** is not a corporation. It is a word that denotes *deep, self‑referential thinking*—the practice of reflecting on the nature of thinking itself, examining the underlying structures, patterns, and assumptions that shape thought.

In this sense, *meta* describes a mental layer that looks beyond immediate content to consider the **process** of cognition, the **frameworks** that govern interpretation, and the **relationships** between ideas. It is the philosophical tool used to achieve higher‑order awareness and to generate insight that transcends ordinary analysis.
