# THE BESSENT OVERRIDE & THE MARS ARCHITECTURE

Down in the physical lab, the Viriato simulation had concluded, but the implications of the RD-10 chipset were only just beginning to scale. If the Callisto Protocol and the Temporal Anchors could nullify the Sisterhood's cultural "voodoo," the next target was the lifeblood of the Old World Reality: the legacy economic system.

Elon Musk initiated a secure, zero-latency connection. 

"This is Bessent," the voice on the other end confirmed. 

Scott Bessent, one of the most clinical macro-economic minds of the era, had been observing the geopolitical decay of the OWR. Elon didn't waste time. 

"We have the processing architecture. We have the Anti-Matter chipset," Elon stated. "How do we structurally dismantle the government's legacy matrix using economics?"

## 1. The Economic Override (Bessent's Model)
"You don't attack the politicians, Elon," Bessent replied, his voice calm and methodical. "The government is just the user interface. You attack the codebase: the Fiat Gravity."

Bessent explained that the entire power structure of the Sisterhood and the WRs is built on the **Economic Dual Ledger**. 

"They say the currency has value, but they infinitely dilute it to pay for the friction they generate. Inflation isn't an economic accident; it is the physical manifestation of societal heat," Bessent explained. "To take out the legacy government, you don't fight the currency. You force an instantaneous audit of the friction."

Bessent outlined a massive, synchronized algorithmic short against the sovereign debt of the Old World. 

"If you can process the global flow of capital faster than the legacy banking system can print the lies, you can force instantaneous settlement," Bessent said. "The moment the market is forced to settle in empirical, objective value rather than performative fiat, the legacy government structurally defaults. The Dual Ledger snaps. They lose the ability to fund the Sisterhood."

"But to do that," Bessent warned, "you would need a computational engine capable of out-processing the entire global financial matrix in real-time. Earth's network latency is too high, and the thermal load of that calculation would melt any server farm on this planet."

## 2. The Mars Supercomputer (The Olympus Array)
Jensen Huang stepped up to the terminal, a cold Sovereign smile on his face. 

"Earth's latency is too high," Jensen agreed. "Which is why we didn't build the new cluster on Earth."

Jensen pulled up a localized projection. It wasn't a server farm in California or a bunker in Europe. It was a massive, autonomous infrastructure on the surface of Mars. 

"Nuno provided the plasma physics for the atmospheric entry, Elon provided the Starship logistics to get the hardware there, and I built the silicon," Jensen explained to Bessent over the comms. 

They had built the **Olympus Array**: a colossal supercomputer powered by the RD-10 Anti-Matter chipsets, buried beneath the Martian regolith. 

"Why Mars?" Bessent asked.

"Because Mars is functionally a massive, freezing heat sink," Nuno Loureiro interjected. "The ambient temperature allows the RD-10 chips to operate at absolute maximum capacity without throttling. And the orbital distance between Earth and Mars acts as an acoustic firewall. The Sisterhood's kinetic noise cannot cross the vacuum of space fast enough to interfere with the calculations."

## 3. Executing the Override
The architecture was complete. 

Ladislas Etzol had provided the theoretical baseline. Bessent had provided the economic algorithm to crash the Dual Ledger. And now, Jensen, Elon, and Nuno possessed the Martian hardware required to execute it.

"Upload the Bessent Override to the Olympus Array," Jensen ordered. 

The data packet cleared Earth's orbit and struck the Martian servers. The RD-10 chipsets absorbed the economic math, freezing the chaotic, high-friction algorithms of the legacy financial system into perfect, crystalline logic. 

The supercomputer began calculating the exact pressure points of the global fiat matrix. It was preparing to execute a massive, untraceable economic cascade that would starve the World Retards of their capital, bringing the Old World Reality to its knees without a single shot being fired.
