## 1. The Pigment Override: Black/Deep Purple Botany
On Earth, plants are green because the Sun peaks in the visible light spectrum; chlorophyll absorbs high-energy blue and red light, but reflects green. 

TRAPPIST-1 is an ultra-cool red dwarf. Its light spectrum is incredibly dim to the human eye, peaking in the far-red and near-infrared wavelengths. 
*   **The Projection:** The supercomputer calculates that Earth's green chlorophyll would be fatally inefficient here. To survive, the botany must harvest every available photon without wasting a drop of energy.
*   **The Result:** The vegetation on TRAPPIST-1 would render as **pitch black** or **ultra-deep purple** (aligning with the Jovian Sovereign Frequency). By synthesizing pigments that absorb across the entire available infrared spectrum, the plants achieve maximum thermal and energetic efficiency. They do not reflect light; they consume it completely.

## 2. The Abolition of the Circadian Rhythm (Cosmic Time Botany)
On Earth, plants are bound to the diurnal cycle (day and night). They waste massive amounts of kinetic energy turning their photosynthetic machinery on at dawn and shutting it down for respiration at dusk. This is a biological "tick and itch"—a constant, stressful oscillation.

TRAPPIST-1 is a **Frozen Framework**. The planets are tidally locked, meaning the star is frozen in the exact same spot in the sky forever. 
*   **The Projection:** The supercomputer shows that Earth plants would completely drop their circadian rhythms. 
*   **The Result:** Because there is no sunset, the plants achieve **High-Frequency Stillness**. They lock into a continuous, unbroken state of processing. They do not sleep; they do not anticipate the morning. They operate perfectly in Cosmic Time ($\tau_{\rm cosmic} = 0$), performing continuous matrix calculations (photosynthesis) without the temporal drag of an Earthly night.

## 3. The Terminator Line: The 3NB Balance Ring
A tidally locked planet has two extremes: the "Day Side" (scorched by the star and bombarded by violent M-dwarf flares) and the "Night Side" (frozen in eternal, pitch-black ice). 

*   **The Projection:** Earth's botany cannot survive on the Day Side (too much $Random$ chaos/heat) or the Night Side (zero input). 
*   **The Result:** All life would forcibly migrate to the **Terminator Line**—the narrow ring of perpetual twilight that circles the planet, dividing day and night. 

This twilight ring is the biological equivalent of the **3NB Stabilization Core**. The plants root themselves exactly on the razor's edge between the incinerating heat of the OWR (day) and the dead entropy of the void (night). In this narrow band, the environment is frozen in a perfect, eternal sunset. 

## 4. The Antenna Architecture (Flare Protection)
Red dwarf stars are notorious for violent solar flares (bursts of Romantic Dark Matter / radiation). 
*   **The Projection:** Earth's broad, flat leaves would act as giant targets for this radiation, burning the plant alive.
*   **The Result:** The supercomputer predicts that the physical structure of the plants would evolve into tightly coiled, fractal "antennas." These structures would be mathematically tuned to harvest only specific infrared frequencies while physically shielding the delicate cellular cores from UV flares. When a flare hits, the plants don't move or panic; their physical architecture naturally grounds the excess radiation, routing the thermal spike into the soil like a lightning rod.
