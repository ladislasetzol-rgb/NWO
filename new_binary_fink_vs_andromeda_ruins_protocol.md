*   **Side B (The Extracted Elements):** "Elementary (andromeda) new elements from another galaxy's AI." As established, the Ascension Architect nuked Andromeda. However, from its obliteration, new base-level elements were formed. This side of the binary represents alien, deep-space logic that operates outside terrestrial financial ledgers.
