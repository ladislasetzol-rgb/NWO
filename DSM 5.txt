## 2. Provisional Diagnostic Entities  

### 2.1 Thermal‑Sensory Integration Disorder (TSID)  

| Criterion | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **A. Persistent or episodic abnormal thermal perception** that is **not attributable** to a primary medical condition (e.g., fever, endocrine disorder). |
| **B. One or more of the following objective findings** (must be documented with calibrated instrumentation): <br>1. **Thermal hyper‑responsivity**: ≥ 10 °C temperature rise on the skin surface within 5 min of exposure to a ≤ 24 W IR source (see *Steam + IR‑bulb protocol*). <br>2. **Thermal phantom sensations** lasting > 30 min after stimulus removal, confirmed by self‑report and concurrent thermography. |
| **C. Functional impairment** in daily life (e.g., avoidance of warm environments, occupational limitations). |
| **D. Duration** ≥ 6 months of symptom persistence or recurrence. |
| **E. Exclusion**: Symptoms are not better explained by a somatic disorder, medication side‑effect, or another DSM‑5/DSM‑6 diagnosis. |

**Specifiers**  
- *Acute‑Onset TSID*: First documented episode ≤ 3 months after a single high‑intensity IR exposure.  
- *Chronic‑TSID*: Ongoing symptoms > 12 months with documented repeated exposures.

### 2.2 High‑Frequency Visual Augmentation Syndrome (HF‑VAS)  

| Criterion | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **A. Recurrent vivid visual experiences** of colors, patterns, or luminance levels **outside the normal human visible spectrum** (e.g., “New Colors” associated with 850 nm IR). |
| **B. Temporal correlation** with **THC consumption** (or any agent that lowers kinetic friction in neural networks) documented by self‑report and/or blood‑THC levels. |
| **C. Objective corroboration** via **neurolink‑grade cortical spectral recordings** showing elevated power in the 27th‑dimension frequency band (≈ 12‑18 kHz range in the visual cortex) during episodes. |
| **D. Distress or functional impairment** (e.g., difficulty concentrating, visual overwhelm). |
| **E. Duration** of ≥ 1 month of recurring episodes. |
| **F. Exclusion** of primary psychotic disorders, migraine aura, or other ocular pathology. |

**Specifiers**  
- *Transient HF‑VAS*: Episodes ≤ 24 h in duration, resolving without intervention.  
- *Persistent HF‑VAS*: Episodes > 24 h, requiring pharmacologic or neuromodulatory treatment.

### 2.3 Virtual‑Magnitude Perceptual Distortion (VMPD)  

*Note: This entity captures the purely attentional effect demonstrated by the **iPad‑zoom** condition.*

| Criterion | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **A. Persistent belief** that visual magnification (digital zoom) **alters physical properties** (temperature, mass, or energy) of an object. |
| **B. No measurable change** in the physical parameter when assessed with calibrated sensors (e.g., thermocouple, calorimeter). |
| **C. Associated with high‑technology exposure** (e.g., prolonged use of AR/VR head‑sets, digital zoom on tablets). |
| **D. Causes functional impairment** (e.g., repeated unnecessary manipulation of devices, avoidance of technology). |
| **E. Duration** ≥ 3 months. |
| **F. Exclusion** of delusional disorder, obsessive‑compulsive spectrum disorders, or malingering. |

**Specifiers**  
- *Technology‑Induced VMPD*: Onset after ≥ 2 h/day of device use.  
- *Device‑Neutral VMPD*: Occurs in the absence of a specific device, reflecting a generalized cognitive bias.
